Our campuses

Two campuses, one Sandbox summer

Choose between a Quaker boarding school in its own private parkland and a modern London university campus. Both are close to Heathrow and central London, and both have the space and facilities for a brilliant summer.

Leighton Park School Brunel University of London Compare campuses
Reading, Berkshire

Leighton Park School

A Quaker school founded in 1890, set in 65 acres of private parkland with 300-year-old oak trees. It's a campus that's really a park of its own.

Students live, learn, eat and play on one enclosed site, so younger children in particular have lots of space to explore while staying safe. It's calm, green and welcoming, and only a short trip from Heathrow and London.

Talk to us about Leighton Park

A park of its own

A private, closed campus in 65 acres of parkland. Lessons, meals, sports and evening activities all happen within the grounds, with space to roam and no busy roads to cross.

A Quaker ethos

Leighton Park is guided by the Quaker values of simplicity, peace, integrity, community, equality and sustainability. It's a natural home for our approach: every voice is heard, everyone is included, and kindness comes first.

Premium meals at a family-friendly price

Meals are freshly cooked on site from farm-assured meat, sustainable fish and seasonal produce, often from local suppliers. Because we work directly with the school, we can offer this standard of catering at a discounted rate.

Food at Leighton Park

Proper food, cooked fresh every day

Hearty soups, wholesome main courses, a salad bar, fresh fruit and puddings students look forward to. Vegetarian, halal and allergy needs are catered for. Just tell us at your consultation.

Uxbridge, west London

Brunel University of London

A modern, single-site university campus in west London. Students get a taste of university life, with everything they need in one place.

Accommodation, classrooms, dining and sports facilities are all within a short walk of each other. It's a great fit for teenagers and our Young Leaders who want a more grown-up, university-style summer.

Talk to us about Brunel

Single en-suite rooms

Modern halls in the heart of the campus, with private en-suite bedrooms, each with a desk, wardrobe and storage.

Top sports facilities

Indoor athletics, sports halls and outdoor pitches for tournaments, clubs and our end-of-week Sandbox Cup.

London on the doorstep

About 20 minutes from Heathrow by road, and around 40 minutes to central London on the Underground from nearby Uxbridge station.
