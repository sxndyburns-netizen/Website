Information for groups

The programme

One programme, tailored to every student

Every student joins the same residential programme: 15 hours of English lessons a week, a full activity programme and two excursions a week. On site, we group students by age and English level, so each student learns and socialises with the right peers.

How groups work

  • A short placement test before arrival and a speaking check on the first day
  • Students grouped by age and English level, in small classes
  • Groups reviewed during the course, so students can move up when they are ready
  • Activities and independence that suit each age group
  • Stays of one to six weeks

What every week includes

  • 15 hours of interactive English lessons
  • Afternoon and evening activities every day
  • Two excursions, each with free time and a packed lunch
  • Residential accommodation and three meals a day
  • Staff on site 24/7, first-aid trained, with a 24/7 emergency line

Locations

London and the Thames Valley

Our summer 2028 venues will be secure residential campuses in London and the Thames Valley, all within easy reach of Heathrow and central London. We will confirm venues ahead of the first summer.

London

University-style campuses

Modern, self-contained campuses close to Heathrow, with central London's sights within easy reach. Especially popular with teenagers and older students.

Thames Valley

Green, secure school campuses

Traditional boarding schools in their own grounds, with fast links to London and Oxford nearby. Especially popular with younger students.

Every venue will offer secure residential accommodation with rooms separated by age and gender, proper classrooms, and space for sport and activities. Airport meet-and-greet and transfers can be arranged.

About us

Founded by Alexander Burns

Alexander has spent several summers delivering English summer programmes, with one aim: to make each one as enjoyable and rewarding as possible for every student.

He founded Sandbox English to offer a better experience at a reasonable price: small classes, engaging activities, great excursions and attentive care. As a small, founder-led school, we avoid the overheads of a large organisation and put our resources where students notice them.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

When does Sandbox English Summer School start?

Our first summer is 2028. Book a consultation to register your interest. We will keep you updated as dates and venues are confirmed.

Why don't you publish prices?

Length of stay, group size, excursions and travel arrangements all affect the cost, so we tailor every quote. Book a free consultation and we will send a clear, written quote with no obligation.

What happens in a consultation?

It's a relaxed video or phone call with Alexander. We will talk about the students, their English level and interests, and the excursions you would like. Then we will follow up with a tailored proposal and quote.

How long can students stay?

Between one and six weeks. We will help you choose the right length of stay at your consultation.

What level of English does my child need?

None at all. Students of every level, from beginners to advanced, are welcome and are placed in a group that suits them.

Where will the course take place?

At residential venues in London and the Thames Valley, all within easy reach of Heathrow and central London. We will confirm venues ahead of summer 2028.

Can you arrange airport transfers?

Yes. We can arrange meet-and-greet and transfers from the airport. Share your travel plans at your consultation.

Do students need a visa?

Many nationalities can come to the UK for a short course without a visa, but some will need a Standard Visitor visa or an Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA). Please check the latest requirements on GOV.UK. We can provide a letter confirming the booking.
