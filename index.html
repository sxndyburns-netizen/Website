Why Sandbox?

A sandbox is where you try things out

We named our school after the place where children first learn to build, experiment and play without fear of getting it wrong. That's how we think languages are best learned.

Our lessons get students talking from the very first hour. Every morning's English feeds into the afternoon's activities, so new words get used straight away: on the sports field, in the drama studio or on a treasure hunt through a medieval city.

Our story & approach

Talk first

Communicative lessons with lots of speaking time, not endless worksheets.

Safe & cared for

Enhanced DBS checks, trained welfare staff and a 24/7 emergency line.

A world of friends

We balance nationalities in every class and every bedroom corridor.

Measurable progress

Placement test, weekly feedback and a CEFR-referenced certificate.
