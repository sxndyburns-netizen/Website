The programme

One programme, grouped for every student

Every student follows the same carefully designed programme. On arrival, we place students in groups by age and English level, so lessons and activities suit each of them.

How the programme works

Our founder

Founded by Alexander Burns

Alexander has spent several summers delivering English summer programmes, with one aim: to make each one as enjoyable and rewarding as possible for every student.

He founded Sandbox English to offer a better experience at a reasonable price: small classes, engaging activities, great excursions and attentive care, without the overheads of a large organisation. Parents, agents and group leaders deal with him directly, from the first conversation to the final day.

Excursions

Two excursions a week, tailored to you

Students take two excursions every week. We choose from a wide range of destinations based on what parents, agents and group leaders would like. Every trip includes supervised free time and a packed lunch. Popular options include:

London walking tour

A guided walk past Big Ben, Westminster Abbey, Buckingham Palace and the South Bank.

  • Free time
  • Packed lunch
  • Staff-led

Oxford tour

Historic colleges, the Radcliffe Camera and the city's famous dreaming spires.

  • Free time
  • Packed lunch
  • Staff-led

Cambridge tour

King's College, the Backs and the riverside of one of the world's great university cities.

  • Free time
  • Packed lunch
  • Staff-led

Harry Potter Studio Tour

Warner Bros. Studio Tour London: the real sets, costumes and props from the films.

  • Free time
  • Packed lunch
  • Staff-led

Madame Tussauds London

The world-famous wax museum, with film stars, sporting heroes and royalty.

  • Free time
  • Packed lunch
  • Staff-led
