Family-run

Started by summer-school staff, run by a family

We've been the teachers, activity leaders and house parents at other summer schools. We loved the job, but we kept seeing the same things: huge groups, tired timetables and prices that kept going up.

So we started our own. Sandbox Languages is family-run. The people you speak to at your consultation are the same people on campus all summer. We put the effort into what students actually remember, like brilliant activities, great food and staff who know every child by name. We keep costs low everywhere else.
