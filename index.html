A family business

Founded by summer-school professionals

Our founders spent many years as teachers, activity leaders and house parents at established UK summer schools. They saw how much better the experience could be, and how much less it could cost.

Sandbox Languages is the result. As a family-run school, we are personally involved in every summer, from your first consultation to the final departure. We invest in what matters most to students and parents: excellent teaching, engaging activities, good food and dedicated staff. Our low overheads let us keep fees fair.
