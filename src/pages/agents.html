For agents

Working with agents

We aim to be responsive, transparent and flexible, and easy to work with.

  • A direct contact: you deal with the founder, Alexander Burns
  • Tailored proposals and clear, written quotes
  • Excursions and activities shaped around your families' wishes
  • Stays of one to six weeks, for students aged 8–17
  • A location around London, or in London on request
  • At least one member of staff for every 10 students

How partnership works

Commission, agent terms, booking deadlines and payment schedules are agreed with each partner during a consultation. We will confirm everything in writing before you promote the programme.

For group leaders

Bringing a group

We plan your group's stay with you: the English focus, the activities and the excursions your students would enjoy most.

  • Students placed in classes that suit their age and English level
  • Two excursions a week, chosen with you from a wide range of options
  • Free time and a packed lunch on every trip
  • 24/7 staff and emergency line, so you are never on your own

Arrangements for group leaders

Leader accommodation, leader places, and how responsibilities are shared between group leaders and our staff are agreed in advance during your consultation, and confirmed in writing.

FAQs

Questions from agents and group leaders

Do you work with agents on commission?

Yes. Commission and agent terms are agreed individually during a consultation and confirmed in writing.

Can you take closed groups?

Tell us about your group in a consultation. We will discuss numbers, ages, dates and excursions, and send a tailored proposal.

How are excursions chosen?

There are two excursions a week. We plan them with you from a wide range of destinations, such as a London walking tour, Oxford, Cambridge, the Harry Potter Studio Tour and Madame Tussauds. Every trip includes free time and a packed lunch.

Where will the course take place?

In the area around London, or in London on request, so you can choose what suits your students. We will confirm your venue ahead of summer 2028, and partners will hear first.
