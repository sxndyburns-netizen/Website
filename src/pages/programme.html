The programme

One programme, tailored to every student

Every student joins the same residential programme, which combines 15 hours of English lessons a week with a full activity programme and two excursions a week. On site, we group students by age and English level, so each student learns and socialises with the right peers.

{{photo:lessons|Students seated at desks in a classroom, seen from behind|wide}}

How groups work

The right group for every student

  • A short placement test before arrival and a speaking check on the first day
  • Students grouped by age and English level
  • Small classes, so every student takes part
  • Activities and responsibilities that suit each age group, with more independence for older students
  • Groups reviewed during the course, so students can move up if they are ready

How we teach

Lessons that students enjoy, and remember

Lessons are active and communicative. The English students learn in the morning is put to use straight away in the afternoon's activities and on excursions.
