Locations

London and the Thames Valley

Our summer 2028 venues will be in two of the best-connected areas of the UK, both close to Heathrow and to the country's most famous sights. We are finalising venues now and will confirm them well ahead of the first summer.

London Thames Valley What every venue offers
London

University-style campuses in London

Modern, self-contained campuses give students a taste of university life, with everything they need in one place.

  • Close to Heathrow for easy arrivals and departures
  • Central London's sights within easy reach by Underground or coach
  • Modern classrooms, sports facilities and residential accommodation
  • Especially popular with teenagers and older students
Thames Valley

Green, secure school campuses in the Thames Valley

Traditional boarding schools set in their own grounds offer a calm, safe base with plenty of space to play and explore.

  • Enclosed grounds with room to roam
  • Fast rail links to London, and close to Heathrow
  • Oxford and Windsor close by for excursions
  • Especially popular with younger students and first-time travellers

Our standards

What every Sandbox venue will offer

Whichever location you choose, the programme, care and standards are the same.

A secure site

Residential accommodation on a supervised, secure campus, with staff on site 24/7.

Proper classrooms

Bright teaching spaces for 15 hours of English lessons a week.

Space to play

Sports fields and indoor spaces for activities, whatever the weather.

Good food

Freshly prepared meals, packed lunches on excursions and options for dietary needs.

Easy travel

Close to Heathrow, with airport transfers available.

First-aid cover

First-aid trained staff and local medical services close by.

Not sure which area suits you?

We will talk through the options in your free consultation and let you know as soon as venues are confirmed.
