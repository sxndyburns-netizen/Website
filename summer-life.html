Activities

Something for everyone, every afternoon

Students choose from a rotating menu of clubs and whole-school events. Our activity leaders make sure quieter students feel just as included as the confident ones.

Sport

Football, basketball, tennis, swimming, rounders, dodgeball and a very competitive end-of-week Sandbox Cup.

Drama & music

Improv games, musical theatre, band practice, karaoke, and the legendary Friday talent show.

Arts & crafts

Painting, photography, jewellery-making, T-shirt design and a summer mural everyone signs.

STEM

Coding challenges, robotics, egg drops, rocket launches and escape-room puzzles.

Evening events

Discos, quiz nights, international evenings, film premieres and a Great British bake-off.

Chill-out options

Board games, reading corners, yoga and quiet spaces for those who need to recharge.

Excursions

Living with us

A home away from home

Accommodation

Supervised boarding houses at Leighton Park, or single en-suite rooms at Brunel. Always separated by age and gender, with bedding and towels provided.

Food

Three meals a day plus snacks and fruit. Vegetarian, halal and allergy-friendly options are available. At Leighton Park, meals are freshly cooked on site to a premium standard.

Supervision

Live-in house parents, a welfare lead and a 24/7 emergency phone line for parents. Students are supervised at all times, with age-appropriate independence.

Staying in touch

Free Wi-Fi in common areas. We use gentle phone-free times so students stay present, with plenty of time to call home.

Health & wellbeing

Access to an on-site nurse and local doctors. Medication is stored safely and given by trained staff.

Arrivals & transfers

Meet-and-greet at Heathrow, Gatwick and Stansted on Sundays. Unaccompanied minor service available for younger students.
