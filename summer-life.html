Summer life

Outside the classroom is where the magic happens

Afternoons, evenings and excursions are packed with things to do, all in English and all with friends from around the world.

Activities

Something for everyone, every day

Students choose from a rotating menu of clubs and whole-school events. Our activity leaders make sure quieter students feel just as included as confident ones.

Sport

Football, basketball, rounders, dodgeball and an end-of-week tournament.

Drama & music

Improvisation games, karaoke and a talent show to finish the week.

Arts & crafts

Painting, photography, design projects and a summer mural.

Team challenges

Treasure hunts, escape-room puzzles and problem-solving challenges.

Evening events

Discos, quiz nights, international evenings and film nights.

Time to relax

Board games, reading and quiet spaces for students who need to recharge.

Excursions

Two excursions a week, chosen with you

There are two excursions every week, chosen from a wide range of destinations to suit what parents, agents and group leaders would like. Every trip is led by our staff and includes supervised free time and a packed lunch. Popular options include:

London walking tour

A guided walk past Big Ben, Westminster Abbey, Buckingham Palace and the South Bank.

  • Free time
  • Packed lunch
  • Staff-led

Oxford tour

Historic colleges, the Radcliffe Camera and the city's famous dreaming spires.

  • Free time
  • Packed lunch
  • Staff-led

Cambridge tour

King's College, the Backs and the riverside of one of the world's great university cities.

  • Free time
  • Packed lunch
  • Staff-led

Harry Potter Studio Tour

Warner Bros. Studio Tour London: the real sets, costumes and props from the films.

  • Free time
  • Packed lunch
  • Staff-led

Madame Tussauds London

The world-famous wax museum, with film stars, sporting heroes and royalty.

  • Free time
  • Packed lunch
  • Staff-led

Have somewhere else in mind? Let us know in your consultation and we will plan it in where we can.

Food

Good food, every day

Students get three freshly prepared meals a day, plus snacks, fruit and a packed lunch on excursion days. Vegetarian, halal and allergy needs are catered for. Just tell us at your consultation.

Living with us

A home away from home

Staff on site 24/7

Our residential team lives on site and is there around the clock.

24/7 emergency line

Parents, agents and group leaders can reach us at any time during the course.

First-aid trained staff

Staff are trained in first aid, and local medical services are close by.

Accommodation

Supervised residential accommodation, with rooms separated by age and gender.

Staying in touch

Time to call home every day, with phone-free lessons and activities.

Arrivals

Airport meet-and-greet and transfers can be arranged. We'll discuss your travel plans at the consultation.
