Summer life

Outside the classroom is where the magic happens

Afternoons, evenings and weekends are packed with activities, trips and time to relax, all in English and all with friends from around the world.

Your summer homeA traditional boarding-school campus in the English countryside, easy to reach from London.

The campus

Space to learn, play and grow

Students live and learn on a safe, self-contained boarding-school campus with bright classrooms, sports fields and all the facilities they need for a brilliant summer.

  • Modern classrooms with interactive screens
  • Sports hall, playing fields, tennis courts and indoor pool
  • Theatre, art studios and a music room
  • Common rooms with games, books and film nights
  • On-site medical centre and 24/7 staffed reception
  • Secure site with controlled access

Activities

Something for everyone, every afternoon

Students choose from a rotating menu of clubs and whole-school events. Our activity leaders make sure quieter students feel just as included as the confident ones.

Sport

Football, basketball, tennis, swimming, rounders, dodgeball and a very competitive end-of-week Sandbox Cup.

Drama & music

Improv games, musical theatre, band practice, karaoke, and the legendary Friday talent show.

Arts & crafts

Painting, photography, jewellery-making, T-shirt design and a summer mural everyone signs.

STEM

Coding challenges, robotics, egg drops, rocket launches and escape-room puzzles.

Evening events

Discos, quiz nights, international evenings, film premieres and a Great British bake-off.

Chill-out options

Board games, reading corners, yoga and quiet spaces for those who need to recharge.

Living with us

A home away from home

Accommodation

Single, twin and small shared rooms in supervised boarding houses, separated by age and gender. Bedding and towels are provided and rooms are cleaned regularly.

Food

Three freshly cooked meals a day plus snacks and fruit. Vegetarian, halal and allergy-friendly options are available. Just tell us when you book.

Supervision

Live-in house parents, a welfare lead and a 24/7 emergency phone line for parents. Students are supervised at all times, with age-appropriate independence.

Staying in touch

Free Wi-Fi in common areas. We use gentle phone-free times so students stay present, with plenty of time to call home.

Health & wellbeing

Access to an on-site nurse and local doctors. Medication is stored safely and given by trained staff.

Arrivals & transfers

Meet-and-greet at Heathrow, Gatwick and Stansted on Sundays. Unaccompanied minor service available for younger students.
