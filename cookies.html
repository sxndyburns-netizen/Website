Cookie policy

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking of any kind.

Last updated: 25 September 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small files that websites store on your device. They are often used to remember preferences or to track visitors across websites.

How this website works without them

  • We do not use analytics, advertising or social media tracking.
  • Our fonts, images and scripts are hosted on our own website, so no third party is contacted when you visit.
  • We do not store anything else on your device, such as local storage.

Because we don't set cookies, we don't need to ask for your consent, and you won't see a cookie banner.

Links to other websites

If you follow a link to another website, such as a social media page, that website may use its own cookies. Please check its cookie policy.

If this changes

If we ever add cookies or analytics, we will update this page and ask for your consent before setting anything that isn't strictly necessary.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Visit the ICO's guide to cookies to find out more.

Questions

Email hello@sandboxenglish.co.uk. For how we use personal information, see our privacy policy.
