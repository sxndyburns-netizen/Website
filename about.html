About us

We believe the best learning feels like play

Sandbox Languages was founded by teachers who have spent years at summer schools. We wanted to create the one we'd want to send our own children to.

Our story

Small by design, big on care

We saw too many summer schools where children were one of hundreds, where lessons felt like school, and where the activity programme was a coach and a clipboard. We knew it could be better.

So we kept things deliberately small. Every student is known by name. Our teachers plan lessons that connect to the day's adventures, and our residential staff are chosen as much for their warmth as their experience.

The result is a summer where young people take risks with a new language, make friends across cultures, and discover they're more capable than they thought.

Kindness first

Everyone belongs here, whatever their level, background or personality.

Purposeful play

Every game and project has a language goal behind it.

Global friendships

We mix nationalities so English becomes the language everyone shares.

Safety, always

Nothing matters more than the wellbeing of the children in our care.

Safeguarding & welfare

Your child's safety is our first priority

We follow UK safeguarding best practice and keep our policies under regular review. Parents can request our full safeguarding policy at any time.

Request our policies
  • All staff undergo enhanced DBS checks, reference checks and safer-recruitment interviews
  • Designated Safeguarding Lead and deputies on site throughout the summer
  • Mandatory safeguarding, first-aid and welfare training for every team member
  • Live-in house parents, with night-time supervision in every boarding house
  • Clear staff-to-student ratios, adjusted for age and activity
  • Headcounts, buddy systems and uniformed staff on all excursions
  • 24/7 emergency phone line for parents and agents
  • Clear anti-bullying, online safety and code-of-conduct policies

FAQs

Questions parents often ask

What level of English does my child need?

None at all for Junior Explorers and Teen Discovery. We welcome complete beginners through to advanced speakers. Young Leaders is best suited to students at pre-intermediate (A2) level or above. Everyone takes a placement test so they join the right class.

Will my child be with lots of students who speak their language?

We actively manage the nationality mix. Each class and each bedroom corridor mixes students from different countries, so English becomes the natural shared language.

How are students supervised?

Students are supervised 24 hours a day by trained, DBS-checked staff. House parents live in the boarding houses, and staff-to-student ratios range from 1:5 for our youngest students to 1:10 for Young Leaders. Older students may have supervised small-group free time on some excursions, with a check-in point and staff nearby.

Can my child use their phone?

Yes. Phones are allowed during free time, and Wi-Fi is available in common areas. We have phone-free lessons and activities, and younger students hand phones in overnight.

What happens if my child is unwell or homesick?

Our welfare team and on-site nurse look after any student who is unwell, and we'll contact you straight away if needed. Homesickness is normal, especially in the first few days. House parents are trained to spot it early and help students settle in, and we'll keep you updated.

Do students need a visa?

Many nationalities can come to the UK for a short course without a visa, but some will need a Standard Visitor visa or an Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA). We'll send a visa support letter once your booking is confirmed. Please check the latest requirements on GOV.UK for your nationality.

Can you cater for dietary requirements and allergies?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, halal and common allergy requirements can be catered for. Please give full details when you book so our catering team can plan ahead.

What is your cancellation policy?

Deposits are refundable if a visa is refused (with evidence). Our full terms and conditions set out refunds for other cancellations, and we strongly recommend comprehensive travel insurance.

Still have a question? Get in touch and we'll be happy to help.
