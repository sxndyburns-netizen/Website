About us

A better summer school, at a reasonable price

Sandbox English Summer School was founded by Alexander Burns, who has spent several summers delivering English summer programmes. His aim is simple: give every student the most enjoyable and rewarding summer possible, without the high price of larger providers.

Our founder

Alexander Burns

Alexander is a student who has spent several summers delivering English summer programmes. He has seen what makes a summer course truly memorable for students: engaging lessons, well-run activities, great excursions and staff who genuinely care.

He founded Sandbox English to deliver exactly that, at a price families can afford. As a small, founder-led school, we avoid the overheads of a large organisation and put our resources where students notice them.

Parents, agents and group leaders deal with Alexander directly, from the first consultation through to the final day of the course.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

When does Sandbox English Summer School start?

Our first summer is 2028. Book a consultation to register your interest. We will keep you updated as dates and venues are confirmed.

Why don't you publish prices?

Length of stay, group size, excursions and travel arrangements all affect the cost, so we tailor every quote. Book a free consultation and we will send a clear, written quote with no obligation.

What happens in a consultation?

It's a relaxed video or phone call with Alexander. We will talk about the students, their English level and interests, and the excursions you would like. Then we will follow up with a tailored proposal and quote.

How are students grouped?

Everyone follows one programme. On site, students are placed in groups by age and English level, and classes are kept small.

Where will the course take place?

At residential venues in London and the Thames Valley, all within easy reach of Heathrow and central London. We will confirm venues ahead of summer 2028.

Which excursions are included?

Two excursions a week, chosen with you from a wide range of options such as a London walking tour, Oxford, Cambridge, the Harry Potter Studio Tour and Madame Tussauds. Every trip includes free time and a packed lunch.

What level of English does my child need?

None at all. Students of every level, from beginners to advanced, are welcome and are placed in a group that suits them.

Can you cater for dietary requirements and allergies?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, halal and common allergy requirements can be catered for. Please share full details at your consultation.

Do students need a visa?

Many nationalities can come to the UK for a short course without a visa, but some will need a Standard Visitor visa or an Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA). Please check the latest requirements on GOV.UK. We can provide a letter confirming the booking.

Still have a question? Book a free consultation or email hello@sandboxenglish.co.uk.
