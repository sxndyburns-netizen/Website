Agents & group leaders

A flexible partner for summer 2028

Sandbox English Summer School works with agents and group leaders to shape a summer around their students. As a small, founder-led school, we offer a direct line to the person running the course and a programme we can genuinely tailor.

For agents

Working with agents

We want to be the easiest school you work with: responsive, transparent and flexible.

  • A direct contact who knows every student on the course
  • Tailored proposals and clear, written quotes
  • Excursions and activities shaped around your families' wishes
  • Regular updates to share with parents during the course
  • Marketing information and photos to support your promotion
Talk to us about partnering

For group leaders

Bringing a group

We plan your group's stay with you: the English focus, the activities and the excursions your students would enjoy most.

  • Students placed in classes that suit their age and English level
  • Two excursions a week, chosen with you from a wide range of options
  • Supervised free time and a packed lunch on every trip
  • Clear arrangements for group leaders on site
  • 24/7 staff and emergency line, so you are never on your own
