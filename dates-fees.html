    What's included

    One all-inclusive weekly fee

    • 15 hours of English tuition, course materials and certificate
    • Full-board residential accommodation and weekly laundry
    • Afternoon and evening activity programme
    • 2 full-day and 1 half-day excursion per week, including entrance fees
    • 24/7 supervision, pastoral care and emergency support
    • Welcome pack, T-shirt and water bottle

    Not included: flights, travel and medical insurance, pocket money (we suggest £50–£80 per week), and optional extras listed in the calculator.

    Booking steps

    How to book

    1. EnquireSend us an enquiry and we'll reply with availability and a personalised quote within one working day.
    2. ReserveComplete the booking form and pay a £350 deposit (plus the £95 registration fee) to secure a place.
    3. PrepareComplete the online placement test and health & consent forms. We'll send a pre-arrival guide.
    4. Pay the balanceThe remaining balance is due 8 weeks before arrival.
    5. Arrive & enjoyWe'll meet your child at the airport or welcome you on campus.
